# Interview guide: State tourism boards and tourism corporations

> Draft by a free model through the FreeLLMAPI gateway, linted, then read and edited by Claude. Not yet reviewed by the founder and not yet used in a conversation.

## Who this is for
This guide is for founders interviewing state tourism boards and tourism development corporations about how they help visitors understand heritage places in India. You are researching how they find, pay for, and manage visitor understanding efforts.

## Before you start
- Listen more than you speak
- Take their words down exactly as stated
- Use no names in your notes
- Keep the conversation to 30 minutes

## Opening
I'm researching how heritage visitor experience is run across destinations and want to learn from your work. I'm not selling anything, just trying to understand how these efforts actually happen. What you've seen and done is exactly what I need to hear about.

## Questions
1. What does a typical day look like in your role with visitor experience and heritage?
2. Can you tell me about the last time visitors needed help finding or understanding something at a heritage site?
3. What did you do to address that situation?
4. What did that cost in time or money?
5. Who else was involved in making that happen?
6. How was the decision made to take that approach?
7. What had you tried before for similar situations?
8. What got in the way of making that work smoothly?
9. How do you pay for guides, signage, or content at heritage sites?
10. How does a new supplier or project get approved for visitor experience work?
11. Who has to say yes before something like that moves forward?
12. What did you want differently when that last situation happened?

## Commitments to ask for
- A follow-up conversation with a specific date
- An introduction to the person who decides on visitor experience budgets
- Sight of a real document such as a budget line, tender, or proposal template
- A small paid or signed pilot project

## Coding the answers
| Tier | Meaning | Example from this segment |
|------|---------|---------------------------|
| T0 | Opinion | "I think signage is important" |
| T1 | Stated intent | "We plan to improve next year" |
| T2 | Past behaviour | "We hired three guides last season" |
| T3 | Time or reputation commitment | "I'll show you our process next week" |
| T4 | Money or signed pilot | "We have a 5 lakh budget for this" |

## Do not
- Do not pitch, mention products, or name companies
- Do not ask for opinions or use "would you" phrasing
- Do not ask about the future or hypotheticals
- Do not accept compliments as evidence of real problems
