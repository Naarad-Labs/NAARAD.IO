# Interview guide: Temple trusts and heritage-site managers

> Draft by a free model through the FreeLLMAPI gateway, linted, then read and edited by Claude. Not yet reviewed by the founder and not yet used in a conversation.

## Who this is for
This guide is for founders interviewing temple trusts and heritage-site managers. It focuses on how these organizations currently handle visitor understanding, costs, and decision-making.

## Before you start
- Listen more than you speak.
- Take their words down exactly.
- No names in notes.
- 30 minutes.

## Opening
I am researching how heritage visitor experience is run in India. I want to learn from your work managing sites and visitors. Can I ask about your recent experiences?

## Questions
1. What is your role, and what does a typical day look like for you?
2. When did you last see visitors struggling to find or understand a specific part of the site?
3. What did you do about that situation?
4. What did that solution cost in time or money?
5. Who else was involved in handling that visitor issue?
6. How did you decide on that specific approach?
7. What have you tried before for similar visitor confusion?
8. What got in the way of solving that problem?
9. How do you currently pay for guides, signage, or educational content?
10. How does a new supplier or project get approved in your organization?
11. Who must say yes to a new expense or vendor?
12. What did you wish had been different about that last visitor interaction?

## Commitments to ask for
- A follow-up conversation with a date.
- An introduction to the person who decides.
- Sight of a real document such as a budget line, tender, or proposal template.
- A small paid or signed pilot.
Say to ask for at least one before the call ends.

## Coding the answers
| Tier | Meaning | Example from this segment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| T0 | Opinion | "Visitors are confused." |
| T1 | Stated intent | "We plan to hire more guides." |
| T2 | Past behaviour | "We hired two guides last month." |
| T3 | Time or reputation commitment | "I will show you our budget." |
| T4 | Money or signed pilot | "We paid for the pilot signage." |

## Do not
- Do not pitch a product or app.
- Do not ask "would you" or "what if".
- Do not ask about the future.
- Do not accept compliments as evidence.
